I am an undergraduate at [MIT](https://www.mit.edu/), where I study Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence and Decision Making.

My interests center on AI safety and mechanistic interpretability: understanding what is happening inside language models well enough to make them more reliable. Most recently, as an LLM Researcher with the [Cambridge Boston Alignment Initiative's CAMBRIA program](https://www.cbai.ai/), I extended [Ye et al.'s work on prompt injection as role confusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12277) by steering activations to study how a model's internal role representation relates to the success of injection attacks. You can read about that work [on LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/uz9pFutDAT7trygM9/steering-role-confusion).

I am currently a UROP researcher in the [Aerospace Controls Laboratory](https://acl.mit.edu/), where I work with [Jonathan How](http://www.mit.edu/people/jhow/) and [Blake Edwards](https://acl.mit.edu/people/blakete) on improving the planning and perception capabilities of an autonomous ground vehicle. On the planning side, I help debug and extend the vehicle's motion planner and implement new planning features to support more reliable autonomous navigation. On the perception side, I develop features that use RGB-D camera data to improve geometric mapping and add semantic information to the vehicle's map, so that it can represent both the structure of its surroundings and the types of objects or regions within them.

Before MIT, I was a researcher in [MIT PRIMES-USA](https://math.mit.edu/research/highschool/primes/), where I worked with [Maxim Arnold](https://personal.utdallas.edu/~maxim.arnold/) on [polygonal refractive outer billiards](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02936), a discrete dynamical system; the resulting paper is accepted for publication in [The American Mathematical Monthly](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/uamm20). I also spent four years in the [Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at UIUC](https://cee.illinois.edu/), where I worked with [Youssef Hashash](https://cee.illinois.edu/directory/profile/hashash) on neural network methods for contact detection in discrete element simulations. I was a Bronze Medalist at the [USA Mathematical Olympiad](https://maa.org/maa-invitational-competitions/) and a participant in the [Citadel Securities Pathways Program](https://www.citadelsecurities.com/careers/students/).
